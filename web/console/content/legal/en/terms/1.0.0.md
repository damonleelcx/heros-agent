## 1. What the Service does, and what it does not do

The Service reads a software repository you control, identifies the places it calls a large language
model, lets you configure alternatives, measures them, and — where you have enabled it — proposes a
change as a pull request in your own repository.

Four limits are part of the Service's definition rather than caveats on it:

1. **We do not merge your code.** The Service may open a pull request. A person on your side reviews and
   merges it. Autonomous merging exists as a separately governed, budgeted and audited option that is off
   unless you turn it on.
2. **We do not hold a write credential to your repository by default.** In the default delivery path your
   own continuous-integration system opens the pull request using the credential it already has. A hosted
   integration that would hold such a credential is opt-in and off.
3. **We do not provide the language models.** Evaluation calls model providers using **your** provider
   keys, from **your** machine or your CI runner, on **your** provider account.
4. **We make no representation about model outputs.** The Service measures and reports; it does not

### 4.3 Gainshare

Where your plan includes it, **gainshare** is charged at **20% of verified, merged savings** — one US
dollar of verified saving, charged at twenty cents.

Three conditions must all hold before any gainshare charge is raised, and each is enforced in code rather
than by policy:

1. **You have recorded consent to gainshare.** Without it the charge is refused. Consent is revocable.
2. **The saving is in the verified-delta ledger.** A claimed saving that is not there, or is there and is
   not billable, is refused.
3. **The change was merged.** Merge is an observed fact read back from your forge, not inferred from a
   pull request being closed. A pull request you close without merging is billed nothing.

Every gainshare line carries the evidence that justified it — the verified delta and the merge commit —
and you can inspect it in the console.
